Your Badges

How to earn and progress badges?
  1. Weekly Warrior: This badge is based off your streaks and the more consecutive you have the higher the badge grade. Bronze at 2 weeks, Silver at 4 weeks, Gold at 8 weeks.
  2. The Explorer: This badge tracks how many different vendors you've collected from. Bronze at 3 vendors, Silver at 5, Gold at 10.
  3. Hot Shopper: This badge is based off the total bundles you have collected. Bronze at 5, Silver at 15, Gold at 25.
  4. Waste King: This badge tracks the total weight of food rescued in Kg. Bronze at 5kg, Silver at 10kg, Gold at 20kg.
  5. Wallet Watcher: This badge is based off total money saved off the retail price of the bundle. Bronze at £10, Silver at £50, Gold at £100.
  6. Category King: This badge tracks how many bundle categories you have collected from. Bronze at 2 categories, Silver at 4, Gold at 6.
  7. Loyal Shopper: This badge is based off the highest number of collections from a single vendor. Bronze at 5 collections, Silver at 10, Gold at 20.
