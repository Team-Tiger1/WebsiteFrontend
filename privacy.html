Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting user privacy in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

  • The platform collects only minimal, non-identifying user data required for core functionality.
  • No payment details or sensitive personal data are collected.
  • Historical marketplace data used for testing may be synthetically generated.
  • Real-world weather data is used solely for demand forecasting and cannot be used to identify individuals.
  • User data is not shared with third parties.
  • The system follows data minimisation and privacy-by-design principles.
  • Data is processed under the lawful basis of legitimate interest.
  • Users have the right to request deletion of their account, after which all identifiable data will be removed.
