    • Password Credentials
  • Data we collect from vendors:
    • Vendor Name
    • Street Address
    • Postcode
    • Description (optional)
    • Phone Number
    • Email
    • Hashed Password Credentials
    • Category
  • Why we collect this data:
    • We collect user data for authentication
    • We collect supplier information for both authentication and contact reseaons
  • Data is stored while accounts are active, and if an account is inactive then data will be deleted after 1 year.
  • The platform collects only the personal data necessary for core functionality and operation of the prototype.

  • The system follows data minimisation and privacy-by-design principles.
  • Data is processed under the lawful basis of legitimate interest. With our interests being safety and functionality.
  • Users have the right to request the deletion of their account, after which all identifiable data will be removed. This option is found in the manage account page.
